Programs

Glimmer Grove Academy’s Fall 2026 programs are open to students from PreK through 3rd grade, with 4th grade students considered based on Chinese language level. The school follows a blended Chinese-and-Western educational approach and offers Chinese, math, reading, music, art, dance, and enrichment. The Chinese curriculum uses the Ma Liping Chinese program from nursery rhymes through 4th grade.

Program What Students Do Notes
Chinese Program Listening, speaking, reading, writing, songs, stories, picture books, and cultural activities. Leveled by age and Chinese background, using the Ma Liping curriculum.
Pickup Service After-school pickup and transportation to campus. Vehicles include safety seats; required for children under 5 and optional for older children by parent preference.
Specialty Classes Math, reading, music, art, dance, expression, and whole-child enrichment activities. Helps students build interests, confidence, and communication skills beyond language learning.
Academic Support Homework help, reading practice, study habits, and organization routines. Gives students a steady, structured rhythm after school.

Sample Afternoon Schedule

  • 3:00 - 3:45 Arrival, snack, and transition time
  • 3:45 - 4:45 Ma Liping Chinese language and culture
  • 4:45 - 5:35 Homework support, reading, and enrichment

Philosophy

Glimmer Grove Academy aims to provide a warm, orderly, high-quality afterschool learning environment where Chinese learning, academic support, and whole-child development naturally fit into every afternoon.

Services

We offer Chinese classes, pickup service, and specialty classes. Pickup vehicles are equipped with safety seats. Safety seats are required for children under 5 and available for older children based on parent preference.

Safety

Children’s safety is always one of our highest priorities. In addition to safety seats for pickup service, our teachers have received CPR training and are prepared to respond professionally in urgent situations.

Pickup & Safety

Pickup vehicles are arranged based on enrolled families’ needs. Children under age 5 are required to use a child safety seat; for children age 5 and older, families may choose based on their preference. All teachers receive CPR training, and pickup routines prioritize safety, punctuality, and clear communication.

Everyday At Glimmer Grove

Each week, we take real classroom photos and videos to share with parents, helping families see children’s learning and growth as it happens.

Founding-Family Offer

Families who complete registration by August 9, 2026 can receive the founding-family offer: the registration fee is waived.
