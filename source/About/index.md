2013年，在福勒神學院受裝備的嚴來恩牧師因讀到高科技業進軍奧斯汀的報導而看見在Austin植堂的戰略重要性。畢業後在矽谷生命河靈糧堂事奉期間不斷的尋求神是否神對他和師母有植堂的託付，也曾兩次全家到Austin禱告行進並探訪弟兄姐妹。2022年底確定呼召之後，他們於2023年6月從灣區搬到奧斯汀並在嚴牧師家裡開始了奧斯汀生命河靈糧堂的事工。

經過一年多沒有固定場地的日子（這期間使用過旅館會議廳、餐廳、電影院等地方），神在2024年10月帶領他們租下一處6200尺的場地，不單交通便利、能見度高、且有可以坐200人的大堂與6間教室。教會目前穩定聚會近百人，且設有4個小組。由於會眾有1/3為兒童及青少年，教會大力於發展兒少的事工。同時他們也極力發展網路事工，其中教會的YouTube頻道的播放次數近4萬，總觀看時長超過5000小時。教會2025的主題為“揚帆啟航”，期待主在2025年帶領他們在經歷聖靈、兒少和宣教方面進入水深之處。


In 2013, Pastor Stephen Yeem, while studying at Fuller Theological Seminary, perceived the strategic importance of church planting in Austin after reading news about high-tech industry entering Austin. Later, while pastoring at River of Life Christian Church in Silicon Valley, he continued to seek God concerning church planting in Austin. His whole family also went to Austin twice for vision trip. After clearly receiving the call at the end of 2022, they officially moved from the Bay Area to Austin in June 2023 and began the ministry of River of Life Christian Church in Austin (ROLA) in his home.

After slightly more than a year of not having a regular place to meet (they had used hotel conference room, restaurant, theater), in November 2024 the Lord led them to lease a 6200 sq ft space which is convenient, visible, and spacious (main sanctuary can seat 200 and there are 6 classrooms). Currently, the church’s membership is close to 100, with four cell groups. As 1/3 of the congregation is children and teenagers, the church focuses on developing next generation’s ministry. At the same time, they are also vigorously developing online ministry. The church’s YouTube channel has nearly 40,000 views and a total viewing time of more than 5,000 hours. The theme of the church in 2025 is "Set Sail and Go," believing that the Lord will lead them to deep waters, especially in the areas of encountering the Holy Spirit, children and youth ministries, and mission.
